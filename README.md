# Strategic Sales & Margin Optimization Analysis (Excel)

> **Executive Summary:** Analyzed $118M in global sales data to identify a 14% profit margin. Uncovered that 86% of revenue is consumed by expenses, specifically within the "Carretera" and "Montana" product lines.

## 📊 Business Problem
The organization is seeing high revenue but thinning profit margins. Management needs to identify which regions, products, and discount tiers are eroding profitability to optimize the next fiscal year's budget.

## 🛠️ Technical Workflow (Excel ETL)
*   **Data Integrity:** Resolved missing numerical values using relational logic (Gross Sales - Discounts).
*   **Cleaning:** Standardized categorical values and handled nulls to ensure a 100% accurate dataset for Pivot Table analysis.
*   **Analysis:** Utilized **Pivot Tables, VLOOKUPs, and Logical Functions** to segment performance across 5 countries and 6 product lines.

---

## 🔍 Top 3 Strategic Insights

### 1. The Discount Paradox
*   **Finding:** "Medium" and "High" discounts are the most frequent, significantly eroding the 14.2% net profit margin.
*   **Implication:** The sales team is over-relying on price cuts to close deals, rather than value-based selling.
*   **Action:** Implement a **Discount Ceiling** of 10% for the Midmarket segment to protect margins.

### 2. Regional Profitability Gap (France & Germany)
*   **Finding:** Despite moderate revenue, France and Germany show lower-than-average profit.
*   **Implication:** High logistics or tax costs are likely unique to these regions.
*   **Action:** Conduct a supply chain audit in the EU to identify hidden operational costs.

### 3. Seasonality & Inventory
*   **Finding:** Sales surge in **Q4 (Oct-Nov)** but crater in **Q1 (Jan-March)**.
*   **Implication:** High storage costs in Q1 for unsold inventory.
*   **Action:** Introduce "New Year" bundles for underperforming products (Montana/Velo) to clear Q1 stock.
